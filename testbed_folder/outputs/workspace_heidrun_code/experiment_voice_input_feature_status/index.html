2. What the feature is

Voice input changes the speech of the developer into text on the computer, with a Whisper model. The developer holds the push-to-talk button of a composition bar (or clicks it to start and clicks it again to stop), and the text goes at the end of the text of that composition bar. Heidrun Code never sends the text by itself. fact (docs/agents_rules/glossary/voice_input.md)

  • packages/auxiliary/voice_input/: the Rust library that records the microphone with cpal, downloads a Whisper model, transcribes with whisper-rs, and replaces the words of the word replacement list (replace_words). It knows nothing about Tauri.
  • packages/apps/desktop_tauri/src/voice_commands.rs: the seven Tauri commands voice_options, voice_download_model, voice_start_recording, voice_cancel_recording, voice_stop_recording, voice_transcribe_recording, voice_discard_recording. voice_words.rs holds the four commands of the word replacement list.
  • packages/apps/web_frontend/src/: lib/voice_input.ts, stores/voice_input.ts, lib/voice_words.ts, stores/voice_words.ts, components/PushToTalkButton.vue, components/VoiceLevel.vue, components/VoiceRecordingIcon.vue, components/settings/SettingsVoiceInputSection.vue, components/settings/SettingsVoiceWordsEditor.vue.
  • packages/auxiliary/heidrun_code_ctrl/: the file voice_words.json and the command line heidrun_code_ctrl voice_words.
  • Rules: docs/agents_rules/user_interface/window/settings_voice_input.md, settings_voice_words.md, and docs/agents_rules/user_interface/workspaces_tabs_and_panes/voice_input_across_panes.md.

4. Current behavior

From the source files and the rule files of commit 5f27a25: fact

  • The switch "Enable voice input" in the Voice input section of the Settings window is off at first (voiceInput: false in stores/settings.ts). When the switch is off, there is no push-to-talk button, no download, and no microphone.
  • The section offers 20 languages plus "Detect the language", and 12 Whisper models. The first model is base (DEFAULT_MODEL_ID in models.rs), because tiny heard one short sentence wrong. The model downloads into the folder models as <file>.part, then gets its final name.
  • A press of 0.3 second or more is push to talk; a shorter press is a click that keeps the recording until the next press (LONG_PRESS_MILLISECONDS = 300 in lib/voice_input.ts). A recording is never longer than 300 seconds (MAXIMUM_SECONDS in recording.rs).
  • The level bars show the last 2 seconds of the voice (40 bars, one each 50 milliseconds) and the recording time. A small X button cancels recording, the wait, or transcription.
  • One microphone for all panes. A press in another composition bar while one records starts nothing and shows a notification with the tab and the workspace. The recordings wait in one queue for Whisper: orange level bars while waiting, green during transcription. A red microphone icon shows on the tab button and on the workspace buttons while the microphone records. A switch of tab or workspace never stops a recording.
  • "Send" and the key Enter stop a recording, wait for its text, then send.
  • The word replacement list (voice_words.json) replaces whole words, ignores the case, lets the longest entry win, and repeats the passes until nothing changes (at most 10 passes).
  • The logs of whisper.cpp go to logs/whisper.log; the application log records the recording, the transcription, and the model download, without the spoken text.
